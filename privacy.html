This policy explains what data the Estimulación Temprana application (the "App") collects, what it is used for, and what rights you have over it. The App is developed by the Estimulación Temprana team (the "Controller"), based in the Argentine Republic.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your email and, if you sign in with Google, the name and profile picture that Google shares with the App. If you sign up with email, also a password, which is stored encrypted and that we can never see.
  • Baby data: the name (optional) and the date of birth you enter to see the activities that match their age.
  • Progress: which activities you marked as done and in which week.
  • Subscription: the status of your subscription (active, canceled or expired), the plan and its expiration date. We do not receive or store your card details: payments are processed by Google Play.
  • Minimal technical data: the logs our providers generate while delivering the service (for example, the date of a sign-in), needed for account security.

The App does not show ads, does not use third-party tracking or analytics tools, and does not access your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

2. What we use it for

  • Creating and maintaining your account, and signing you in.
  • Showing you activities based on your baby's age and saving their progress across all your devices.
  • Managing the free trial and the subscription, and knowing which content is available to you.
  • Answering your questions and preventing abuse (for example, stopping one person from using several free trials).

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for purposes other than those described here.

3. Who we share it with

Only with the providers we need for the App to work, who process the data on our behalf:

  • Supabase (servers in São Paulo, Brazil): database and account authentication. Privacy policy.
  • Google: Sign in with Google and subscription billing through Google Play. Privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat (United States): purchase verification and subscription status. It receives an internal identifier of your account, not your name. Privacy policy.

Some of these providers are located outside Argentina. They apply security measures and contractual data protection commitments. We may also disclose data if required by law by a competent authority.

4. Where it is stored and how we protect it

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored in the Supabase database, where each user can only access their own information. The App also keeps a copy on your phone so it can work offline.

5. How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. If you delete it, we immediately and permanently erase your account, your baby's data and the progress. We only keep an irreversible code derived from your email (not the email itself) to prevent the free trial from being repeated. Google and RevenueCat keep purchase records according to their own policies and applicable tax obligations.

6. Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your data:

  • From the App: Profile → Delete account erases all your data instantly.
  • By email to estimulaciontemprana.app@gmail.com. We reply within 10 business days.

More details in How to delete your account.

The Agency for Access to Public Information (Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública), as the Supervisory Authority of Law No. 25,326, has the power to handle complaints filed by anyone whose rights are affected by non-compliance with the personal data protection rules in force.

7. Children

The App is intended for adult parents and caregivers, not for children. The baby's data is entered by the responsible adult, who decides what information to provide (the name is optional).

8. Changes to this policy

If we change it, we will publish the new version on this page with its update date. If the changes are significant, we will also let you know inside the App.
