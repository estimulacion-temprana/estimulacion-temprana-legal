From the App

  1. Open Estimulación Temprana and sign in.
  2. Tap the profile icon in the top right corner.
  3. At the bottom of the screen, tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted instantly.

By email

If you no longer have the App installed, write to estimulaciontemprana.app@gmail.com from the email you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 10 business days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email, name, profile picture and password.
  • The baby's data: name and date of birth.
  • All activity progress.
  • The subscription status stored on our servers.

What is kept

  • An irreversible code derived from your email (not the email itself), only so the free trial cannot be repeated.
  • The purchase records kept by Google Play and RevenueCat according to their policies and tax obligations.

Important: your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel your Google Play subscription. To stop paying, cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
